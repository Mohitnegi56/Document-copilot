Enterprise Knowledge Copilot — Sample Policy

Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in the employee's local timezone.

Leave Policy
Annual paid leave: 20 days per calendar year.
Sick leave: 10 days per year; unused sick leave does not roll over.

Security Policy
All company data must be stored in approved cloud drives only.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all internal systems.
Report security incidents to security@company.com within 24 hours.

Onboarding
New hires complete security training within the first week.
IT equipment is shipped 5 business days before the start date.
